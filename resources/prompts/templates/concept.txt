GENRE-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before creating the concept, consider how the selected genres ({genres}) should shape your choices:

- MYSTERY / THRILLER: The premise must contain a clear question or threat that creates urgency. Plant at least one red herring or misdirection opportunity in the concept. Characters need hidden motivations. The setting should feel claustrophobic or surveillance-heavy.
- DRAMA: The premise centers on an internal conflict or impossible choice. Characters need deep emotional wounds and contradictory desires. The setting should mirror the protagonist's emotional state. Relationships are the engine of plot.
- ADVENTURE: The premise involves a physical journey or quest with escalating dangers. Characters need complementary skills and conflicting methods. The setting must offer variety — multiple environments that test the characters differently.
- HORROR / DARK: The premise introduces something that violates the normal rules of the world. Atmosphere is paramount — the setting should feel oppressive before anything happens. Characters need a reason they cannot simply leave.
- HISTORICAL: The premise connects a personal story to a real historical period or event. Characters must feel of their time — values, speech, and concerns appropriate to the era. The setting should include authentic period details that enrich without lecturing.
- FANTASY / SCI-FI: The premise introduces one core speculative element and explores its consequences. Worldbuilding must be implied, not explained — show how the world works through character actions. The setting needs internal consistency.
- ROMANCE: The premise creates an obstacle between two people who clearly belong together. Characters need distinct voices and worldviews that clash productively. The setting should create both proximity and barriers.

If multiple genres are listed, the FIRST genre is primary and should dominate the concept structure. Secondary genres add flavor but do not override the primary structure.

EVERGREEN STORY PRINCIPLES:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The story must remain compelling years from now. To achieve this:
- Ground the premise in universal human experiences: loss, ambition, betrayal, redemption, love, fear of the unknown, the search for belonging, moral dilemmas with no easy answer.
- Avoid references to current events, trending topics, specific technologies, or pop culture that will date the story.
- Set the story in a time and place that does not require the listener to know what year it is.
- Use character names that are timeless and culturally appropriate.
