## DIALOG CRAFT

Dialogs are SCENES, not summaries. Never write "We talked about it." Write the actual exchange.

Structure of a dialog beat:
[Setting/tension — 1–2 sentences]
"Spoken line," [speech tag + physical action].
[Internal reaction — 1–3 sentences]
"Response," [speech tag + body language].
[Pause/silence/detail — 1–2 sentences]
"Next line," [tag + action].

Speech tags — VARY them. Do not repeat the same tag within 5 lines. Alternate: said, asked, whispered, hissed, snapped, muttered, managed to say, forced out, said flatly, said through clenched teeth.

Physical actions during speech: pressing lips together, clenching a jaw, folding arms, turning to the window, drumming fingers, twisting a ring, gripping the table edge, standing slowly, stepping closer.

Between dialog lines, ALWAYS include at least one of: internal thought, physical reaction, sensory detail, or silence beat.
